# Research and capability ledger

Checked 13 October 2026. This is a targeted research synthesis, not a preregistered systematic review. Dates are publication dates where distinguished from online-first. Exact pacing/humour prescriptions are production hypotheses.

## P01 | Conversational personalization
Mayer et al., 2004

https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ684999

Three narrated-animation experiments: conversational wording improved transfer, not retention. Not proof that slang, fake intimacy or constant informality helps.

## P02 | Instructional humour review
Zhou & Lee, 2025

https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11814177/

Review finds a complex, mixed achievement literature; perceived enjoyment and achievement must be separated.

## P03 | Related versus aggressive teacher humour
Learning and Instruction 102, 2026, 102311

https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S095947522500235X

Two randomized video-lesson studies, 206 experts and 325 students. Socio-emotional/motivational ratings improved with course-related humour; do not infer delayed achievement gains.

## P04 | Worked examples and fading
Atkinson, Renkl & Merrill, 2003

https://asu.elsevierpure.com/en/publications/transitioning-from-studying-examples-to-solving-problems-effects-/

Near-transfer support; far transfer is not automatic. Include explicit explanation of principles.

## P05 | Self-explanation
Bisra et al., 2018

https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10648-018-9434-x

Meta-analysis of prompts; specific relational explanations, not simply repeating sentences.

## P06 | Retrieval practice
Yang et al., 2021

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33683913/

Classroom meta-analysis; effects depend on task, feedback and other implementation details.

## P07 | Perceived versus actual learning
Deslauriers et al., 2019

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31484770/

University Physics experiment; passive fluency can produce misleadingly positive feelings of learning.

## P08 | Feedback
Wisniewski, Zierer & Hattie, 2020

https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2019.03087/full

Meta-analysis with substantial variation. Specific information matters; no guarantee from praise alone.

## P09 | Curiosity and memory
Gruber et al., 2014

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25284006/

Trivia/fMRI findings are not a recipe for dopamine-triggering edits or guaranteed exam gains.

## P10 | Prerecorded response prompts
Gaudreau et al., 2020

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33013552/

58 four-year-olds; preschool story-reading cannot establish optimal teen lesson design.

## P11 | Synthetic voice comparison
Dincer, 2022

https://www.ijopr.com/article/the-voice-effect-in-multimedia-instruction-revisited-does-it-still-exist-12116

Modern TTS and human voice did not differ significantly in this study; not proof of equivalence for every task.

## P12 | Synthetic voice enthusiasm
Liew et al., 2023; online 2022

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35967831/

244 university participants; some emotion/load effects, no improved immediate retention or transfer.

## P13 | Attention-span claims
Bradbury, 2016

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28145268/

Review challenges fixed 8-second and 10–15-minute rules; does not deny attention fluctuation.

## P14 | Spacing
Cepeda et al., 2006

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16719566/

Distributed verbal practice synthesis; exact spacing depends on retention goal and material.

## P15 | Analogies
Glynn et al., 1994

https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED378554

Teacher/research resource: explicit mappings and limitations are important safeguards.

## P16 | Multimedia theory
Mayer, 2024

https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10648-023-09842-1

Selecting, organizing and integrating relevant information; not a mandate for constant animation.

## P17 | Irrelevant details
Kienitz et al., 2023

https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10176302/

Experimental seductive-details evidence; relevant visual beauty is not the same as irrelevant decoration.

## P18 | Indian multilingual teaching
Anderson, 2024; online 2022

https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/01434632.2022.2045300

Case study of eight secondary English teachers, not causal evidence for a fixed Hinglish proportion in STEM.

## S01 | Chemical balancing
OpenStax Chemistry 2e, 4.1

https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/4-1-writing-and-balancing-chemical-equations

Scientific check for coefficients, fixed species, atom conservation and simplest ratios; sample script is original.

## S02 | Current and drift
OpenStax University Physics 2, 9.2

https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/9-2-model-of-conduction-in-metals

Doorway analogy is only about flow rate and counting, not signal propagation.

## S03 | Chain rule
OpenStax Calculus 1, 3.6

https://openstax.org/books/calculus-volume-1/pages/3-6-the-chain-rule

Compose rate factors; examples should be independently verified.

## S04 | Enzymes
OpenStax Biology 2e, 6.5

https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/6-5-enzymes

Activation-energy reduction and induced-fit limits to rigid lock/key stories.
