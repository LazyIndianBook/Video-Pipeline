## Assumptions

One10-minute media lesson contains8.5minutes of speech and about7,800characters. Paid generation allowance20%. Premium TTS reference$0.10/1,000characters applies to checked v2/v3 rates, not an asserted v4 quote. STS reference$0.12/generated minute. LLM narration allowance$0.60, automatic QA allowance$0.08 and storage/export allowance$0.05 per lesson.

Review$20/hour, editing$15/hour and performer$30/hour are planning inputs, not verified Indian market rates. Premium review/edit time is25+10minutes. Self-hosted30+15; human recording60+review20+editing20; performance/STS45+review20+editing15; hybrid9+review25+editing10. Hybrid assumes80%TTS and20%human-performed/STS.

Self-hosted example:$0.80/GPU-hour and real-time factor2, unbenchmarked. A factor2 means each second of generated audio requires2compute seconds. Fixed allowance$100–$300/month according to method. An optional$4,000initial narration subsystem implementation reserve is separate; new licensing/buyouts must be quoted.

## Scope exclusions

Full academic source research, animation generation/rendering, platform distribution, audience streaming, taxes, large commercial voice buyouts, purchased GPU hardware and additional locales are not silently included. Native-language review and new complex terminology may require more time than this baseline. A low synthesis price is not evidence that the lesson is good.
