# 10 · Decisions and conflict resolutions

**What this is.** Every **Runbook decision** (RD) made in documents 00–12, in one table. A Runbook decision is made only where the sources conflict or are silent. Each row gives both source values (with where they live), the chosen value, the rationale drawn from the sources, and the event that should trigger a revisit. Rows RD-01 to RD-09 are the conflicts the brief required to be resolved; RD-10 onward are further conflicts or gaps found while writing this runbook. RD-41 to RD-46 were added on 15 Oct 2026 after the end-to-end audit (`../14_end_to_end_audit/AUDIT_REPORT_2026-10-15.md`).

**Rules for changing a decision.** Change it here first, record the evidence (pilot data, quote, evaluation) and the date, then update the documents that cite the RD number. A decision that touches science, voice or layout invalidates the approvals of artefacts made under the old value.
