# ExamLeaf authoring contract

Priority: scientific accuracy → genuine independent learning → accessibility → warmth and clarity → humour and novelty.
Audience: Class 10–12; faceless, diagram-led STEM revision. Do not copy a named teacher's voice, character, catchphrase or personal anecdotes.
Use simple English by default for this sample. Language edition is explicit, not inferred from nationality. Assamese/Hinglish adaptations require audience fit and native editorial review.

## Evidence versus production choices
Retrieval, worked examples, selective signalling and feedback have a research foundation. Effects depend on implementation. Exact joke counts, interaction intervals, word rates and creative devices are unvalidated production settings until tested with ExamLeaf learners.
No script can promise every learner will master any chapter in ten minutes. Missing prerequisites require repair or smaller scope.

## Narrator
A capable, warm teacher who notices the next likely difficulty. Explain the reason for a step. Use contractions, direct questions and varied sentence lengths. Avoid stagey fillers, fake mistakes, constant exclamation marks, generic motivational paragraphs and invented school memories.
Warmth is not pretending to hear, see or know the viewer. Do not say “I heard your answer” in prerecorded content. Conditional feedback is allowed. No score guarantees, identity-based praise, shaming, or dependence on the teacher.

## Each concept
Meaning → representation → precise terminology → worked decision → reduced-support attempt → independent attempt → feedback and delayed retrieval.
A learner's correct reproduction while looking at an answer is not independent performance. Hide the answer in visuals and captions until the response interval ends.

## Analogies and jokes
An analogy records target relationship, familiar relationship, mapping, limitations and return to formal science. No teleology disguised as a literal mechanism.
Humour targets the situation or a misconception, never a student's ability, identity, family, accent, poverty, marks or body. A joke should survive deletion without damaging the explanation. Use zero rather than force one. No unlicensed clips or invented popularity statistics.

## Voice and timing
Keep spoken text, formula display, voice direction, and animation cues separate. Internal tags are not universal TTS syntax. Resolve provider support before synthesis. Insert exact multi-second response pauses in the media timeline; tags do not guarantee duration.
Target ten minutes is a production plan until audio is measured. Do not accelerate dense science to fit. User pauses add study time.

## Release
Unverified sources, scientific implications, pronunciation, translations and any answer marked correct require review. Automated structure checks cannot approve science or humour. Human subject review plus full rendered-video review remain mandatory.
