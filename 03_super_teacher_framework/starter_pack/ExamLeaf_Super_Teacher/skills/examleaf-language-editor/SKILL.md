# examleaf-language-editor

Read `references/authoring_contract.md` before working.

## Inputs
Approved semantic script, language/medium, terminology glossary and native reviewer availability.

## Workflow
Preserve the scientific meaning, numbers, assumptions and task demand across versions. Use one dominant teaching language. Introduce official terms beside their explanation and retain required examination terminology. Put any code-switch at a meaningful boundary, not inside a difficult formula. Do not assume Hindi suits every Assam learner or that Indian students share one slang register. Translate the learning function of humour rather than a pun word-for-word. Mark Assamese drafts for native subject-editor review. Original rhyme is optional, brief, and followed by the scientific rule; no pseudo-traditional attribution.

## Outputs
localized_script.md; terminology.csv; native_review_queue.md

## Acceptance and escalation
Every new scientific claim must have source support or be explicitly marked for review. Maintain immutable IDs for unchanged claims and scenes. Never silently change the model to improve comedy, fluency or timing. Stop release on a critical scientific, accessibility or deceptive-personalization issue. This Skill gives instructions; it is not a permission or publication boundary.

## Evaluation
See `evals/evals.json` for a negative test. Model runs have not been executed in this starter pack. Human read-aloud and student validation are still required.
