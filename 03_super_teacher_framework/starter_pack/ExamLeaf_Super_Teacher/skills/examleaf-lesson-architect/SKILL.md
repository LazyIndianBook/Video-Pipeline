# examleaf-lesson-architect

Read `references/authoring_contract.md` before working.

## Inputs
Source packet with stable source IDs; class, topic, language, learner baseline, duration and exam scope.

## Workflow
Produce lesson_brief.md with one primary independent capability, prerequisites and a source-linked claim ledger. Identify what is deliberately out of scope. Write the assessment before drafting a hook. Choose a phenomenon, misconception or problem that leads to that assessment. Budget fully worked, completion and independent tasks; include a targeted repair route. Use planned timing, not claimed audio timing. Do not quietly turn a revision brief into first teaching of an entire difficult chapter. If the sources are incomplete, mark the gap and scope down rather than inventing content.

## Outputs
lesson_brief.md; claims.json; assessment_key.md

## Acceptance and escalation
Every new scientific claim must have source support or be explicitly marked for review. Maintain immutable IDs for unchanged claims and scenes. Never silently change the model to improve comedy, fluency or timing. Stop release on a critical scientific, accessibility or deceptive-personalization issue. This Skill gives instructions; it is not a permission or publication boundary.

## Evaluation
See `evals/evals.json` for a negative test. Model runs have not been executed in this starter pack. Human read-aloud and student validation are still required.
