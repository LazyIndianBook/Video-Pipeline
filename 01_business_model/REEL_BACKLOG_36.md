## 4. Rules for producing these Reels (from the response, sections 6 and 13, and the Execution Runbook section 5)

All of the following are **launch experiments / proposed starting rules, not algorithm laws** (Meta: ranking uses multiple predictions and signals; no universal Reel length, posting frequency or viral formula is established).

| Element | Proposed starting rule |
|---|---|
| Frequency | Four or five reviewed Reels per week |
| Most clips | 20–45 seconds, one concept or one mistake |
| More complex explanations | 45–75 seconds when needed |
| Hook | A clear question, contradiction, or unfinished task—not unrelated spectacle |
| Cover text | Short, readable, topic-specific |
| Captions | Audience + useful promise + necessary condition + one CTA |
| Music | Optional; never necessary to make the explanation work |
| Distribution | Re-layout and publish suitable material to YouTube Shorts as well |
| Review | Compare qualified visits, attempts, and purchases by topic/format cohort |

Use licensed audio and original visual assets. Do not distort a scientific diagram to follow a trend.

**High-converting Reel structure (response 6.2):** 0-3 s identify the problem; 3-8 s invite a prediction or point out the tempting error; 8-30 s deliver a complete explanation; final seconds offer one relevant next action. Example: Hook "Which line represents the larger resistance? Check the axes." / Payoff: show the comparison at the same voltage / CTA: "Try the three-question graph check." Do not ask viewers to like, follow, comment, save, tag three people, and buy - all in the same clip.

**Truthful titles and thumbnails (response 6.3):**

| Content | Proposed title | Cover/thumbnail |
|---|---|---|
| Electricity | "HSLC Electricity: Read the Graph Before Using the Formula" | **CHECK THE AXES** |
| Algebra | "This Cancellation Can Remove a Valid Answer" | **WHERE DID IT GO?** |
| Chemistry | "Balance the Equation Without Changing the Substance" | **COEFFICIENT OR SUBSCRIPT?** |
| Biology | "Chromosome Count and DNA Amount: Keep Them Separate" | **TWO DIFFERENT COUNTS** |
| Product demonstration | "How One ExamLeaf Question Links to Its Explanation" | **QUESTION -> REPAIR -> RETRY** |

The title must accurately describe what the viewer receives. A 60-second checklist is not "the complete chapter mastered in one minute."

**Do not restrict all Reels to the free chapters.** A useful misconception correction from a paid chapter can stand alone and lead to a relevant preview, worksheet, or product demonstration.

**Auditable content record (Runbook section 5) - one row per Reel in your tracker:** chapter ID, claim source, science approval, script, 9:16 scene, caption, target class/medium, CTA destination, experiment ID, release status. No automatic publication of unreviewed science, misleading claims, or generated testimonials. Every Reel needs one complete useful payoff and one next action; a related free lesson or independent challenge is usually better than asking for a purchase before any teaching. Re-layout scientific scenes for vertical viewing rather than simply cropping out labels (do not crop a horizontal animation into a Reel if it removes axes, conditions, units, or necessary context).

**Content-to-Reel workflow (response section 13):** approved chapter and question IDs -> long-form explanation -> select one useful misconception/question -> re-layout into vertical composition -> generate caption and one matching CTA -> scientific + editorial approval -> schedule publication -> aggregate performance by topic and destination.

**Experiments to run on this backlog (response 15.2):** compare a prediction-first opening with a worked-example opening using comparable topics and destinations; define the primary outcome before each test; measure retained sales and contribution, not only clicks; do not announce a winner after two purchases or one unusually viral post.
