# Eleven v4 Path B audition, 16 Oct 2026 (BLOCKED on the Free plan; re-run after upgrade)

**Outcome of the first attempt (16 Oct 2026).** All twelve requests were refused with HTTP 402 `paid_plan_required`: ElevenLabs does not let Free accounts use shared-library voices through the API. The premade voices (American, British, Australian English) are the only ones the Free plan can synthesise, and none is a valid candidate for en-IN or hi-IN. No audio exists in this folder yet and nothing was billed. `manifest.json` keeps the exact texts, voice ids and request ids so the run can be repeated unchanged once a paid plan is active.

Twelve clips: three library voices (Devi and Raju, Hindi; Ziina, Indian English) × two beats (the M1 hook and one explanation line) × two locales (en-IN as `en`, hi-IN as `hi`), generated with `eleven_v4` through the Text to Dialogue route on the founder's Free-plan account with a fixed `seed`. `manifest.json` records every request (text, characters, request id, bytes, latency, SHA-256).

**Status.** Audition material only. No voice is admitted: the native-speaker gate (GL-voice, VOICE registry §3.7) has not run, no `voice/` entry exists, and the Hindi lines are the lead's drafts in NCERT terminology that need native review before any other use. The English lines are the project's own worked-example text (VOICE §5). Free-plan audio is MP3 at 44.1 kHz/128 kbps; production needs the plan that allows PCM and commercial use (verify terms).

**How to rate (two native raters per locale, independently, before discussing).** For each clip score 1–5 on: (1) pronunciation of the critical terms (ohm, resistor, volt, voltage, current, proportional, potential difference, ohmic, constant temperature; Hindi: ओम, रेज़िस्टर, वोल्ट, धारा, विभवांतर, समानुपाती, ओमीय चालक, प्रतिरोध); (2) naturalness of the Indian-English or Hindi delivery (no foreign vowel colouring, natural stress); (3) pace against the l9_10 band (about 130–155 wpm; no rushing on the question); (4) register fit for the Super Teacher persona (warm, precise, curious without hype); (5) artefacts (clicks, breath, mid-word glitches). A voice passes the audition for a locale only with no score below 3 and a mean of 4 or more from both raters; it is then run through the golden set `gs/<locale>-<family>` before a `voice/` entry is written.

Record the ratings in `ratings.csv` (columns: file, rater, q1, q2, q3, q4, q5, note).
